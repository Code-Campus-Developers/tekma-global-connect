# TEKMA Global Partners Corporate Website

## Objective
Build a production-quality, true multi-page corporate website for TEKMA Global Partners Limited, centred on the supplied official logo and its navy, blue, green, gold, magenta and purple visual language.

## Pages
- Home
- About Us
- Oil & Gas
- Acreage & Asset Brokerage
- Marine & Vessel Brokerage
- Commercial Real Estate
- Consultancy
- Leadership
- Global Opportunities
- Contact Us
- Privacy Policy
- Terms of Use
- Custom 404 state

## Experience and Design
- Establish a premium editorial visual system using navy, white and petroleum blue as the foundation, with restrained logo-colour accents.
- Use the official uploaded logo unchanged in the header, footer and brand moments, plus a padded square favicon derived from the same file.
- Create cinematic energy, maritime, property and leadership imagery tailored to each key page.
- Build a sticky desktop header with accessible Services menu and CTA, plus a refined mobile navigation drawer.
- Add restrained global-network motifs, world-map connections, section reveals, image movement and interactive states, all respecting reduced-motion settings.
- Design every layout intentionally for desktop, tablet and mobile with accessible contrast, focus states and comfortable controls.

## Shared Components
- Site header, services menu, mobile menu and footer
- Hero and page hero treatments
- Breadcrumbs and section headings
- Service, value and leadership cards
- Image-and-copy editorial sections
- Global reach visualisation and connection diagram
- Reusable CTA sections
- Contact and opportunity forms with client-side validation and clear success/error feedback
- Buttons, fields, selects, textareas, upload control and toast feedback

## Content and Trust
- Rewrite all supplied material into polished British corporate English while preserving every stated fact.
- Avoid invented metrics, deals, offices, licences, awards, clients, listings, partnerships and guarantees.
- Clearly frame TEKMA as a facilitator of credible introductions and transaction engagement, not as a provider of regulated legal, escrow, securities or financial services.
- Use a dignified portrait placeholder treatment for the chairman until a real portrait is supplied.

## Technical Implementation
- Create one TanStack route per page with shared site chrome in the root layout.
- Add unique titles, descriptions, Open Graph metadata, Twitter card metadata and self-referencing canonicals to every content page.
- Include appropriate Organisation, ProfessionalService, BreadcrumbList and Person structured data using only supplied facts.
- Prepare crawler files without inventing an unpublished production domain.
- Optimise generated media, lazy-load non-critical images and retain minimal client-side JavaScript.
- Keep forms private in the interface; submissions will be validated locally and acknowledged in-page, without persistent delivery until a backend destination is provided.

## Verification
- Check the full route set and navigation on desktop and mobile.
- Test menus, CTAs, both forms, validation, keyboard focus and reduced-motion behaviour.
- Review every page visually for overflow, clipping, hierarchy and image quality.
- Confirm the project builds cleanly and the preview has no runtime or console errors.
